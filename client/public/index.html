Merge: grant proposals, written together

Merge is grant-writing software for nonprofits, consultants, and solo grant writers. It turns a funding application into a shared checklist: split the RFP into questions, assign each one, keep every answer inside its word limit, reuse past answers, get sign-off, and merge everything into one narrative you can download as PDF or Word.

What Merge does

  • Questions, not documents. Each funder question becomes a task with an owner, a status, and a word or character limit.
  • Compliance built in. Counts update as you type so every answer fits before submission day.
  • Answer bank. Every saved answer is searchable, and Merge suggests similar past answers while you write.
  • Send for review. Share a read-only link; a board member or executive director approves or requests changes with no account.
  • Team approvals. Route a finished proposal to an approver and keep a paper trail.
  • Ask Merge. A writing assistant that knows your organization profile, partners, and the project you have open.
  • AI reviewer. A funder's-eye critique against the grant website and purpose statement.
  • Partners, past proposals, file cabinet, grant calendar, and per-grant notes.
